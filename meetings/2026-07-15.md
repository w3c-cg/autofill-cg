* Jon: Agree with what Stephen said. Semantics just tell you what the thing is, not how you should interact with it.

* Jon: Also if you get semantics right, as an autofill provider we're incentivized to use those semantics. It makes our lives much easier, the experience less risky, etc.

* Jon: I was always bugged about “autocomplete” as a sibling to autofill, but it’s not exactly the same thing. Would it be better to better describe what autofill is looking for?

  * Maybe that's too nitpicky? But I keep coming back to it.
